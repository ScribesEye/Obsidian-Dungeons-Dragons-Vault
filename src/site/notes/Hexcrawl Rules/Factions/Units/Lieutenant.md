A Lieutenant is a member of a faction that is trusted by that factions leader for their competence and ability and is allowed to lead forces and rule over parts of their land. 

A faction will have multiple lieutenants, usually at least two lieutenants always stay by the leaders side, whilst any additional lieutenants lead forces or rule over the factions [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|forts]]. A faction can only have as many forts active and maintained as it has lieutenants, although most often a faction has two more lieutenants than forts at all times. If a faction has fewer lieutenants, one or more forts are designated as unsupervised and loses 1d4 troops every time the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] for that faction is rolled. 

Some lieutenants are given charge of furthering one of the factions goals and can be encountered outside their territory. All lieutenants can be befriended, fought and/or killed by players as well as by events on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]]. Lieutenants can also be promoted from events on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]], increasing the number that faction has.
