A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction struggles with a brief period of desertion. Until the completion of the next [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Clock Cycle\|Clock Cycle]] remove 1d4 enemies from all encounters with this faction.
