A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction recruits a new [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenant]] and bolsters their forces. Add one new lieutenants to the faction and for add 1d4 enemies to all encounters with this faction until the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Clock Cycle\|Clock Cycle]] is complete.
