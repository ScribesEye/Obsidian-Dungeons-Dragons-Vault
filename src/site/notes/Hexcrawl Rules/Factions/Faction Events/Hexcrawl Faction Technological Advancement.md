A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction discovers a new technology. Choose one fort or outpost that serves as the basis of this technological discovery and related production. Until this outpost or fort is [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Faction Events/Hexcrawl Faction Raid\|raided]] or destroyed the faction gains 1 additional [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Faction Events/Hexcrawl Faction Conquest\|conquest point]] every time they roll the conquest result on the faction clock. 
Additionally add 1d6/3 (rounded down), enemies using this technology to any encounter with this faction whilst until the related outpost or fort is raided and or destroyed.
