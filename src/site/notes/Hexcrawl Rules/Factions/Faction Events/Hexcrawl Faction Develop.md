A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction builds a new [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] or upgrades an outpost to a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] if the faction has one or more spare [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenant]] and at least two outposts remaining afterwards. 

#### Construction Time
Once this results is rolled, designate one hex in the factions territory that the outpost is to be built in or where the fort is to be upgraded. These projects are completed once the triggering [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Clock Cycle\|Clock Cycle]] on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] is completed, before which other factions can disrupt the process via raids and conquest and players can also discover and prevent the completion of the project.
