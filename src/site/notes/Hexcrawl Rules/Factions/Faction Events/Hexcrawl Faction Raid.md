**Want to rework**
A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction raids a nearby faction. To determine which faction is raided roll a d8 with 1 being north. The faction will then attempt to raid one [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] that is within 3 hexes of their border and that does not have a fort closer to the the border than it. 

The faction then rolls a d20, with a outpost needing an 11 or higher and a fort needing a 16 or higher in order to succeed with the raid. If successful the faction steals the blueprints for the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Faction Events/Hexcrawl Faction Technological Advancement\|technology]] being used in that location and/or one notable treasure as well as 30% of the total gold and rations in the location. 

If the target is the players faction and the players attempt to intervene, roll 2d10 to determine the size of the raiding force, which on a successful raid burns down one production building and steals its treasure, gold and materials.
