A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the relevant faction conquers a certain amount of nearby hexes from another faction.
### Conquest Dice and Conquest Points
Upon rolling this outcome on the event dice, roll the conquest dice, 2d4, to determine the conquest points (CP) for the conquest. If the resulting CP is 2, roll a d20, 15 or above one of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenants]] of the conquering faction is injured, on a 14 or below the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenant]] dies. If the resulting CP is 8 the conquering faction a new [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenant]] is found and an enemy [[Hexcrawl Rules/Factions/Units/Lieutenant\|Lieutenant]] is killed or injured, perform the same d20 rolls as above. 
### Conquest Requirements
To be able to conquer a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]] a faction needs to control at least one adjacent hex, the hex contains an [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] the faction needs to control at least 2 adjacent hexes, if the hex contains a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] the faction needs to control 4 or more adjacent hexes. 
### Conquest Cost
The type of terrain and structures on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]] determine the amount of conquest points it costs to conquer a hex. An [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] increases the cost by 1 and a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] increases the cost by 2.

| Terrain  | Base Cost | Outpost | Fort |
| -------- | --------- | ------- | ---- |
| Plains   | 1         | 2       | 3    |
| Desert   | 1         | 2       | 3    |
| Forest   | 1         | 2       | 3    |
| Hills    | 2         | 3       | 4    |
| Mountain | 3         | 4       | 5    |
