A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction explores nearby hexes. To determine in which direction the faction explores roll a d4 with 1 being north, then roll 2d6 to determine the number of hexes explored followed by a Wisdom (Perception) to determine the expeditions ability to discover hidden locations. 
Until the completion of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Clock Cycle\|Clock Cycle]] the party can encounter the explores and loot their records, learning of any findings.
