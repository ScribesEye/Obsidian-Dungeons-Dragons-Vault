A result of the Event Dice of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] simulating that the faction engages in ineffective border skirmishes with another faction. To determine which faction border the conflict takes place in roll a d8 with 1 being north. Until the completion of the next [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Clock Cycle\|Clock Cycle]] remove 1d4 enemies from all encounters with this faction.
