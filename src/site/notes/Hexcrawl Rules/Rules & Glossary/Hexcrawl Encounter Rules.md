### Triggering Encounters
Most encounters will either be triggered by [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]] or play out as specified by the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]]. In the latter case see the notes for how the encounter is triggered or found, what it contains and relevant materials needed to run it. If triggered by an event clock, see the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Exploration Encounter Table\|Exploration Encounter Table]] if it was triggered by the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Overclock\|Overclock]] or the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Dungeon Encounter Table\|Dungeon Encounter Table]] if triggered by the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Underclock\|Underclock]]. 
For all events not triggered by the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] roll a (d8), on a 1 roll an additional encounter, (additionally roll 1d4 to determine during which hour of the current [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl Watch\|watch]] this encounter occurs, on a 1 or result that has already passed for the current watch, resolve both encounters at the same time - alternative, use 12 on 2d6 for roll twice).
#### Lairs and Tracks
If an encounter is triggered while exploring or in a dungeon via an encounter table, some encounters can have a chance to be replaced with finding the tracks of the encounters creatures or finding it in their lair. First set a percentage chance to encounter the creatures tracks and a chance to encounter their lair and add this to the encounter table. To resolve this roll a d100 at the same times as rolling on the table and refer to the percentages specified on the encounter table.
#### Reactions
If the attitude the participants of the encounter have towards the party is not clear or determined by previous events, then roll on the reaction table to determine their attitude. To determine the reaction and attitude the participants have towards the party, and/or each other if needed, roll 2d6 and refer to the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Reaction Table\|Reaction Table]]. 

| 2d6   | Reaction             |
| ----- | -------------------- |
| 2-3   | Immediate Attack     |
| 4-5   | Hostile              |
| 6-8   | Cautious/Threatening |
| 9-10  | Neutral              |
| 11-12 | Amiable              |
### Generating and Replenishing Encounters
Most encounters cannot be ran the same way twice, this means they will have to be updated, changed or replenished. All generated encounters should be reviewed using the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Encounter Checklist\|Encounter Checklist]].
##### Hexkey Replenishing  - NEEDS WORK
Most [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] notes should be about a specific geographical location not a specific event or creature. This means that as the encounter is resolved the location can be used again as new creatures or anomalies occur or react to the party's actions.

Replenishing the encounter on the tile can be done by thinking what would happen or allowing inspiration from the players actions guide the world reaction to the events that took place there. Sometimes however, a dice can be used to help or if it's unclear what would happen after the players leave. 
*If the location was within the territory of a faction roll a d6, on a 1 something else occupies or finds the location the location before the faction. On a 2, something happens to change the structure or details of the location before the faction finds it. On a 6 the faction finds track or learns details about the party. Otherwise the faction finds and attempts to begin reclaiming the territory*

| Roll | Consequence                                                                                      |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | New occupants (Adjacent Faction / [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Wandering Exploration Table\|Wandering Exploration Table]] / [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Dungeon Encounter Table\|Dungeon Encounter Table]]) |
| 2    | Geographical or Magical alteration (Hazard)                                                      |
| 3    | Faction Recaptures Location                                                                      |
| 4    | Faction Recaptures Location                                                                      |
| 5    | Faction sets up a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Trap\|Trap]] in the location                                                       |
| 6    | Faction learns details about party or their tracks (Pursuit or Counter)                          |
