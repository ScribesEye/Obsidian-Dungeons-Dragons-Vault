|Trailblazing is a special exploration pace which can be taken in conjunction with other travel paces. Trailblazing halves the traveling speed of the party but also marks an efficient [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl Trail\|Trail]] through the wilderness with some form of signs – paint, simple carvings, cloth flags, etc. 

Entering a hex while trailblazing provokes an additional roll of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Overclock\|Overclock]] [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Clock Dice\|Clock Dice]]. Once blazed, this trail is considered a known trail to the party. A party can also choose to Trailblaze a hex they are in as if exploring that hex, this also provokes a roll on the Overclock.

When blazing a trail, the character making the signs can make a Wisdom (Sleight of Hand) check to disguise them so that they can only be noticed or found with a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check. The result of the Wisdom (Sleight of Hand) check is the DC to find the trail.
