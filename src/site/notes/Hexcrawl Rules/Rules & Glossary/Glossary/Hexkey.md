A Hexkey is a a code or number attached to a specific [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]] on a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]] that contains information about the hexes terrain type, notable features, any locations, events or encounters that exist on the hex. 

In general a Hexkey should contain information about the hex as a geagraphical location where events and encounters can take place, such as a cabin, statue, canyon, castle or magical anomaly. The aim of this design is to ensure that once the players have traveled over a hex new content can be generated as new inhabitants occupy or use the location or new events occur. For example the players loot a dungeon but leave the corpses behind, leaving ample materials for a necromancer to occupy the dungeon and raise a small army of undead.
