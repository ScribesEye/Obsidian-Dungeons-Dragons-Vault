A hexmap is a map of either a World, Continent or Region overlayed with a grid of [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hexes]] that is used when running a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl\|Hexcrawl]]. On the hexmap each hex should be numbered or given a specific [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] that contains information about the hex and any location or event tied to the hex. 

When running a hexcrawl two hexmaps should be used. One hexmap is used by the Game Master and contains all hexkeys and information about locations and terrain. The other map is used by the players and contains empty and unfilled hexes that the players over time can fill in with information. The player version of the map contains notable features such as mountains, rivers and major structures.
