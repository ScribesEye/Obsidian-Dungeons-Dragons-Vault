A location that offers an especially good view of the nearby land. From a vista a character can see several miles/[[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|hexes]] into the distance and learn about its terrain and any obvious locations or structures. 

The distance a character can see from a vista is stated in its [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] and can be reduced by [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Weather Conditions\|Weather Conditions]].
