This table describes the time it takes to [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Enter]] a hex at a normal traveling pace at 30 feet speed. It also the DC of the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Getting Lost\|Navigation Check]] to not the lost when entering the tile as well as the DC of any [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Forage Check]] made in the hex.

| Terrain Type    | Fast Pace | Normal Pace | Slow Pace | Navigation DC | Forage DC   |
| --------------- | --------- | ----------- | --------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| Road            | 1 hour    | 1 hour      | 2 hours   | 10            | See terrain |
| Mountain Road   | 2 hours   | 4 hours     | 6 hours   | 10            | 16          |
| Desert          | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 12            | 20          |
| Plains          | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 12            | 12          |
| Hills           | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 14            | 12          |
| Moor/Dunes      | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 14            | 16          |
| Frozen/Tundra   | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 14            | 18          |
| Forest (Sparse) | 1 hour    | 2 hours     | 3 hours   | 14            | 14          |
| Forest (Medium) | 2 hours   | 4 hours     | 6 hours   | 16            | 14          |
| Forest (Dense)  | 2 hours   | 4 hours     | 6 hours   | 18            | 14          |
| Jungle          | 2 hours   | 4 hours     | 6 hours   | 16            | 14          |
| Swamp           | 2 hours   | 4 hours     | 6 hours   | 15            | 16          |
| Mountains       | 4 hours   | 8 hours     | 12 hours  | 16            | 18          |
