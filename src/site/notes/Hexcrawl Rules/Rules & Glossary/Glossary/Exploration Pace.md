An expedition’s speed is based on the speed of its slowest member and describes the time it takes the party to [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Enter]] a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]].
- In 1 hour at normal pace, the expedition can travel a number of miles equal to its speed divided by 10.
- At a fast pace, the expedition can travel 150% of its normal speed (round down to full hours).
- At a slow pace, the expedition can travel two-thirds of its normal speed.

Additionally a party can adopt 3 different paces when adventuring:
##### Fast
When entering a hex with a known trail, the party can move quickly through the wilderness, parties traveling at fast pace:
- Cannot use Stealth checks to avoid detection.
- Suffer disadvantage to Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (investigation) checks.
- Suffer a -5 penalty to navigation checks.
- Rolls the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Clock Dice\|Clock Dice]] of any [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]] with disadvantage (subtract highest)
##### Normal
An expedition traveling at normal pace cannot use Stealth checks to avoid detection.
##### Slow
While moving at a slow pace, the party is purposely being careful. An expedition traveling at slow pace:
- Gains advantage on navigation checks.
- Can make Stealth checks to avoid detection.
- Rolls the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Clock Dice\|Clock Dice]] of any [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]] with advantage (subtract lowest)
##### Optional Rule - Trailless Fast Pace
Parties can also travel at a fast pace without following a trail, when do doing so parties:
- Cannot use Stealth checks to avoid detection.
- Suffer disadvantage to Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (investigation) checks.
- Suffer a -5 penalty to navigation checks.
- Rolls double the amount of [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Clock Dice\|Clock Dice]] for any [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]] and makes the rolls with disadvantage (subtract highest)
- Cannot Trailblaze.

The base travel distances for a party with a traveling speed of 30 feet. For time to enter hex refer to the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl Terrain Table\|Hexcrawl Terrain Table]].

| **Pace** | **Per Hour** | **Per Watch** | **Per Day** |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------- | ----------- |
| Fast     | 4.5 miles    | 18 miles      | 36 miles    |
| Normal   | 3 miles      | 12 miles      | 24 miles    |
| Slow     | 2 miles      | 9 miles       | 18 miles    |
