A **watch** is the basic unit for tracking time. A watch is equal to 4 hours.
#### Determining Time Within a Watch
To randomly generate a particular time within a watch, use 1d8 to determine the half hour and 1d30 to determine the exact minute (if necessary).
#### Types of Watches
There are six watches per day and four types of watches:
- Active
- [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Hexcrawl Resting Rules\|Rest]]
- Travel
- Camp
While traveling, it is generally assumed that a party is spending two watches per day traveling, at least two watches per day resting, one watch setting up and packing down camp and one watch per day engaged in other activities, represented by the active watch.
### Watch Actions
During a usual traveling day there is one watch, the active watch, that a character can use as they wish, provided they didn’t march beyond 8 hours. Here are some examples of downtime activities they could spend their time on:
##### Forage 
Characters can forage during an active watch. Foragers make a Wisdom (Survival) check against the Forage DC of the terrain. On a success, the forager either gains 1 ration of food or finds a source of fresh water (allowing the party to drink their daily ration of water and for waterskins to be refilled). An additional ration of food or source of fresh water can be found for every 2 points by which the check result exceeds the DC. A food ration found by foraging can be used to cook a meal for one character.
##### Cook
Characters can take the time to prepare a full warm meal using rations foraged during the same day. Each ration consumed allows one character to recover 1 additional hit dice during their next long rest.
##### Craft Mundane Item
Characters can Craft Mundane Items during an active watch, reducing the time remaining to craft an item they’re working on by half a day, provided they have sufficient resources.
##### Craft Magic Item
Characters can Craft Magic Items during an active watch, reducing the time remaining to craft an item they’re working on by half a day, provided they have sufficient resources.
##### Cover Tracks
A character can attempt to cover the party’s tracks. This is a stealth action, that can be taken if the party is moving at a slow pace. The action can also be taken during an active watch to attempt to cover any tracks left behind in the current hex (including from the camp). The character makes a Wisdom (Stealth) or Wisdom (Survival) check to set the Track DC for any pursuer attempting to find or follow their tracks. The check suffers disadvantage in very soft ground, but gains advantage on hard ground. Condition modifiers apply normally to the pursuer’s tracking check
##### Find Tracks
A Character can attempt to find tracks by making a Wisdom (Perception) check against a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Track DC\|Track DC]] to attempt to follow creatures or find trails leading into nearby hexes. 
If successful the tracker can make a Wisdom (Survival) check against the track DC to attempt follow it, making a new roll each time a new hex is entered. 
If a character is attempting to follow their own tracks they make the checks with advantage.
##### Scout Ahead
A character can scout ahead to attempt to learn more about the lay of the land and help chart a course. If the navigator of the party receives a scout’s report or the scout uses the information themselves, they gain advantage on their navigation checks for the next travel watch (optional, change to +3 bonus).
##### Improve Camp
A character can spend an active watch gathering nearby materials to increase the comfort of the camp to improve the quality of rest. A character taking this action makes a Wisdom (Survival) or relevant tools check against the Forage DC of the hex, on a success each character gains one hit dice upon completing a long rest in the camp. 
##### Optional Rule - Sighting
A character can spend an active watch finding a good outlook in the current hex and attempt to learn more about the nearby hexes. A character taking this action makes a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check to attempt to find a good location, on a success they can see the terrain type of the hexes beyond the adjacent hexes, if they fail they only see into the adjacent hexes. The character can attempt to make a Wisdom (Perception) check to discover any notable features in the adjacent hexes but takes a -5 penalty to the check due to their distance.
##### Short Rest
A character can spend their Active Watch taking a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Hexcrawl Resting Rules\|Short Rest]] (See below)
