A Hexcrawl is a system for travel using a map overlayed with a grid of [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hexes]] to quickly calculate distance as well as travel time and cost. This map is referred to as a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]]. A Hexmap can be split into two categories of scale; [[Hexcrawl Rules/Appendixes/World Hexcrawl Appendix\|World]] maps and [[Hexcrawl Rules/Appendixes/Regional Hexcrawl Appendix\|Regional]] maps. 

In a hexcrawl the players choose which hexes they navigate and explore and the Game Master decides what events or encounters happens during the exploration, mostly with the aid of the hexes [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]].
