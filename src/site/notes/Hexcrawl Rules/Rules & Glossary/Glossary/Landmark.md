A landmark is notable geographical feature such as a river, spire, oddly shaped mountain, massive tree or tall structure. It can help explorers set their course, find their way and can often be seen from several [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hexes]] away. Each landmark will have its own features and distance from which it can be seen as stated in their [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]].
#### Identifying Landmarks
If the PCs are unsure of a landmark but have had previous experience with it or heard about it, it may be possible to identify it with a Wisdom (Survival) check, at the DM’s discretion. The accuracy and detail of the identification will depend on prior experience.
#### Navigating Using Landmarks
Traveling in a straight direction towards a landmark provides a +5 bonus to any [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Getting Lost\|Navigation Check]] made to [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Enter a Hex]]. Traveling in any other direction whilst in the vicinity of a landmark provides a +3 bonus to any navigation checks made to enter a hex.
#### Reorienting Using Landmarks
If a party realizes that they have [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Getting Lost\|Gotten Lost]] whilst within sight of a landmark they can use their check made when entering a hex to attempt to make a Wisdom or Intelligence (Survival) check to identify which hex they are in against the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl Terrain Table\|Naviagation DC]] of the current hex.
