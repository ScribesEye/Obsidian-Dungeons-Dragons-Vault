Alternate resting rules can be used for a hexcrawl if the goal is to challenge the players in combat without having to use multiple encounters per day to drain resources or run high deadliness in each encounter. This reinforces the value of resource management in simpler encounters and tactics to preserve the health of characters. For the hexcrawl this means that what and how you chose to explore comes at a greater opportunity cost and becomes more meaningful.

While exploring characters can gain the benefits of one Short and one Long Rest every 24 hours. Due to the challenging conditions that travel and exploration incurs good rest is harder to achieve than at a settlement. Some abilities such as innate abilities from a characters species recover at dawn every day rather at the end of a long rest, reflecting their source being nature. 
#### Short Rest
A short rest takes one Watch (4 hours) to complete and is interrupted by rolling initiative, taking damage or casting a spell with a spell slot. After completing a short rest characters may:
- Spend a hit dice to roll the dice and regain hp equal to the result plus the characters Constitution modifier.
- Regain a use of an ability that recovers on a short rest (option, half or full proficiency bonus uses).
- Spend a hit dice to recover proficiency bonus amount of uses of short rest abilities. (Details at the bottom).
Taking a short rest will most likely provoke a roll on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Overclock\|Overclock]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Underclock\|Underclock]]depending in where it is taken. 
#### Long rest
A long rest takes two Watches (8 hours) 6 of which must be spent sleeping and 2 of which may be light activity. To gain the benefits of a long rest it needs to be taken in conjunction with the Camp Watch Action otherwise it becomes a poor quality long rest (see below). After completing a long rest a character may:
- Regain up to half of their maximum hit points.
- Reduce their level of exhaustion by 1.
- Regain all uses of their abilities that recover on a short rest.
- Spend one hit dice to roll the dice and regain hp equal to the result plus the characters Constitution modifier.
- Spend a hit dice to regain a number of uses equal to their proficiency bonus of abilities that recover on a long rest. (Details at the bottom)
Taking a long rest will most likely provoke rolls on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Overclock\|Overclock]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Underclock\|Underclock]] depending in where it is taken. 

If a long rest is taken in a secret or hidden location characters gain the following additional benefits:
- Recover a number of hit dice equal to their character level divided by 4 rounded down, minimum 1.
If a long rest is taken in an allied [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] they gain the following improved benefits instead:
- Regain all your missing hit points.
- Recover a number of hit dice equal to half their character level rounded down, minimum 1.

If a long rest is taken in an allied [[Hexcrawl Rules/Sanctuary/Sanctuary\|Sanctuary]] a character gains the following benefits:
- Regains all their missing hit points.
- Regains all their missing hit dice.
- Regains all uses of their abilities that recover on a short or long rest.
- Reduce their level of exhaustion by 1.
##### Poor Quality Long Rest
Long resting without taking a camp watch, represented by going to sleep without sufficient shelter and food, leads to poor recovery. After completing a poor quality long rest a characters may:
- Spend a hit dice to roll thedice  and regain hp equal to the result plus the characters Constitution modifier.
- Regain one use of an ability that recovers on a short rest (option, half or full proficiency bonus uses).
- Spend a hit dice to recover proficiency bonus amount of uses of short rest abilities. (Warlock 1 slot equals one hit dice, paladin 1 use of lay of hands equals 5hp).
- Spend ***one*** hit dice to regain a number of uses equal to their proficiency bonus of abilities that recover on a long rest.
### Hit Dice Ability Recovery
When taking a short or long rest characters may spend a use of their hit dice to regain a number of uses of abilities that recover on a short or long rest equal to their proficiency bonus. If there is any overflow from recovering one resource that recovery can be used to recover other resources as well, any recovery not used at the end of the rest is lost. Below is an overview of how this interacts with different abilities from the different classes.
##### Spell Slots
Regain a number of spell slot levels equal to the characters proficiency bonus. For example a wizard at level 3 regains 2 spell slot levels that they can spend to regain two 1st level spells slots or on 2nd level spells slot.
Warlocks spend one hit dice per spell slot.
##### Limited Use Resource (Wild Shape)
Regain a number of uses equal to the characters proficiency bonus.
##### Point Resources (Sorcery & Ki Points)
Regain a number of points equal to the characters proficiency bonus.
##### Lay on Hands
One use of lay on hands is counted as 5 hit points.
##### Resource Dice (Battlemaster, Second Wind)
Regains a number of dice uses equal to the characters level.
