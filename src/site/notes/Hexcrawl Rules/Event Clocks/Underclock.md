The Underclock is an [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clock]] used in [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl\|Hexcrawl]] to trigger events/encounters while the players are exploring locations on the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]] such as a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Dungeon\|Dungeon]] or a hostile [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]]. The triggered events/encounters should be dangerous and/or hostile to reflect the risk of exploring these locations. For dungeons giving a name to the rising sense of dread and dangerous adventurers feel while exploring them is a good idea to ground the mechanic in the world and reduce the sense that it is there to obstruct the players. 

*""The Underworld is not just a basement or a cave.  The Underworld is a place that hates you.  It is hostile architecture.  It hates you in a way that only the blind tonnage of stone and cold air can have.  It hates your lively blood.  It hates the sunshine warmth still lingering on your skin.*

*Live there long enough, and the Underworld can learn to tolerate you.  You will grow pale and cold and strange, like the other inhabitants of that place.  The long years will render you smooth and inoffensive, like a pearl held in the mouth.  The Underworld's irritation fades and scabs over.*

*But this doesn’t apply to delvers.  They dig greedily and they dig fast.  They are hated the most, and this hatred is felt as soon as the Underworld is entered.""*
### Clock Cycle
The Underclock starts at 20 and counts down to 0 using a dice. Once the clock goes below zero an event or encounter is triggered, usually using a random table, and the clock then resets to its starting value, starting a new *Clock Cycle*.
### Clock Dice
As the players explore a location roll a *Clock Dice*, at the beginning this is a d6. As the party takes Rests the clock dice can increase or decrease in size in the following order of steps, d6 <-> d8 <-> d10 <-> d12 <-> d20.
### Rolling the Clock Dice
The Clock Dice is rolled when the party completes an adventuring turn, taking about 10 minutes, for example the following actions provoke a roll:
- Exploring a new Room
- Moving through 3 already explored Rooms
- Lingering or Searching (Each player gets one action/check)
- Making noise (Kicking down a door - Combat does not provoke a roll)
- Take a Rest, see below.
### Core Mechanics
The Underclock and its Dice has the following mechanics:
1. The dice explodes. If you get its *maximum value* (a 6 on a d6), immediately roll the dice again and subtract the combined value from the Clock
2. If the Clock _drops below 0_, an Encounter is triggered.  After the Encounter resolves, the clock resets.
3. If the Clock _equals 0 exactly_, it resets to 3. This represents that the either the participants of the event/encounter have been distracted by something else.
4. If the Clock _equals 3 exactly_, a Foreshadowing Event occurs. So the clock reaches 0, it bounces up to 3 and a Foreshadowing Event occurs.  (These are just omens/spoor/clues, more or less.)
### Resting
Different types and locations of rest interact with the Underclock differently.
#### Short Rests
Short rests interact with the Overclock the following ways:
- A Short Rest provokes 2 rolls of the Clock Dice and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards if you take it in a well traveled location.
- A Short Rest provokes a special roll of the Clock Dice if you take it in a  that is not hidden or secret., roll the Clock dice twice and subtract the highest result from the clock and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- A Short Rest provokes no roll if you take it in a secret or hidden location but increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
#### Long Rests
Long rests interact with the Overclock in the following ways:
- A Long Rest in a well traveled location provokes 3 rolls of the clock dice and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- A Long Rest Provokes no roll of if you take it in a secret or hidden location but increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- Everything in-between provokes 1 or 2 rolls of the clock dice (default to 2 if in doubt) and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- Any Long Rest outside and not in an [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]], [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Dungeon\|Dungeon]] decreases the size of the clock dice by 1 step.
- A Long Rest in a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Sanctuary/Sanctuary\|Sanctuary]] resets the the clock dice to a d6 and resets the clock cycle, setting the clocks value to 20.
