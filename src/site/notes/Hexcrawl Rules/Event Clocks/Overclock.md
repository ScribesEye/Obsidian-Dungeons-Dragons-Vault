The Overclock is an [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clock]] used in a[[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl\|Hexcrawl]] to trigger events/encounters while the players are exploring the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]]. 
### Clock Cycle
The Overclock starts at 20 and counts down to 0 using a clock dice. Once the clock goes below zero an event or encounter is triggered using the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Exploration Encounter Table\|Exploration Encounter Table]], and the clock then resets to its starting value, starting a new *Clock Cycle*.
### Clock Dice
As the players explore the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]] roll a *Clock Dice*, at the beginning this is a d6. As the party takes Long Rests the clock dice can increase or decrease in size in the following order of steps, d6 <-> d8 <-> d10 <-> d12 <-> d20.
### Rolling the Clock Dice
The Clock Dice is rolled when the party takes certain actions, for example the following actions provoke a roll:
- [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Entering]] a new [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]]
- Moving through 3 already explored [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hexes]]
- [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Exploring]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Trailblaze\|Trailblazing]] a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hex]]
- Take a Rest, see below.
### Core Mechanics
The Overclock and its Dice has the following mechanics:
1. The dice explodes. If you get its *maximum value* (a 6 on a d6), immediately roll the dice again and subtract the combined value from the Clock
2. If the Clock _drops below 0_, an Encounter is triggered.  After the Encounter resolves, the clock resets.
3. If the Clock _equals 0 exactly_, it resets to 3. This represents that the either the players or the participants of the event/encounter have been distracted by something else.
4. If the Clock _equals 3 exactly_, a Foreshadowing Event occurs. So the clock reaches 0, it bounces up to 3 and a Foreshadowing Event occurs.  (These are just omens/spoor/clues, more or less.)
### Resting
Different types and locations of rest interact with the Overclock differently.
#### Short Rests
Short rests interact with the Overclock the following ways:
- A Short Rest provokes a roll with disadvantage on the Clock Dice if you take it in a well traveled location, roll the Clock dice twice and subtract the highest result from the clock.
- A Short Rest provokes a regular roll if you take in a location that is not hidden or secret.
- A Short Rest provokes no roll if you take it in a secret or hidden location.
#### Long Rests
Long rests interact with the Overclock in the following ways:
- A Long Rest in a well traveled location provokes 3 rolls of the clock dice and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- A Long Rest Provokes no roll of if you take it in a secret or hidden location.
- Everything in-between provokes 1 or 2 rolls of the clock dice (default to 2 if in doubt) and increases the size of the clock dice 1 step afterwards.
- A Long Rest in a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Outpost\|Outpost]], [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Fort\|Fort]] or [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Dungeon\|Dungeon]] decreases the size of the clock dice by 1 step.
- A Long Rest in a [[Hexcrawl Rules/Sanctuary/Sanctuary\|Sanctuary]] resets the the clock dice to a d6 and resets the clock cycle, setting the clocks value to 20.
