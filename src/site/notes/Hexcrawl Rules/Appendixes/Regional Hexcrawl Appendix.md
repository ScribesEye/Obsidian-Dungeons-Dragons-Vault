This document serves as an appendix for rules and mechanics related to Regional [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexcrawl\|Hexcrawl]] and Settlement/Sanctuary Development. It also includes a brief description of the intent behind the mechanics. 
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Regional Hexcrawl Rules\|Regional Hexcrawl Rules]] 
The goal of the regional hexcrawl is make exploration into an engaging gameplay loop and quickly calculating distance, expenses and time for travel. Compared to the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Appendixes/World Hexcrawl Appendix\|World Hexcrawl]] the [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hex\|Hexes]] are smaller and some hexes should be left empty apart from basic terrain in order to not slow down the pace of progress too much. The [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexmap\|Hexmap]] in a Regional Hexcrawl can also be more detailed.
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Hexcrawl Generation Rules\|Hexcrawl Generation Rules]]
These rules aim to simplify and support generating what is in a hex. The main focus is on resources and potential locations for safe resting or future construction.
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Appendixes/Hex Keys Appendix\|Hex Keys Appendix]] 
For each hexmap a matching set of [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Glossary/Hexkey\|Hexkey]] should be generated for easy access during sessions. These keys are linked to a hex and describes what is in said hex and holds information about any discoverable events, encounters or locations and how to run them. 
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Rumor Table Rules\|Rumor Table Rules]]
To create additional reasons for characters to leave the sanctuary and venture out into the wilderness rumors and quests can be used. Quests can be about finding a certain location and acquiring information, interfering with a faction or obtaining a magical relic. Rumors on the other hand are just that, mentions of and stories about what might exists out in the wilderness meant to stir the imagination of those who hear it. Rumors can invite greed, wonder, a chance at glory or a sense of duty depending on their contents.
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]]
To create a sense of suspense, meaning to the passage of time and triggering events and encounters different types of [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Event Clocks\|Event Clocks]] can be used. These clocks count down at random intervals using a dice as the players explore their environment or rest, eventually leading to an event or encounter being triggered. These clocks can also be used to simulate events and change in the world as well as what actions the factions in the hexcrawl take as the adventure goes on. 
The [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Overclock\|Overclock]] is used to generate events happening while the players explore the hexmap.
The [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Underclock\|Underclock]] is used to to generate events happening while the players are exploring a dungeon or specific location such as a fort or outpost.
The [[Hexcrawl Rules/Event Clocks/Faction Clock\|Faction Clock]] is used to simulate the actions the factions on the hexmap take and their effects as the players explore and rest. 
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Hexcrawl Encounter Rules\|Hexcrawl Encounter Rules]]
There should multiple types of Encounters in a hexcrawl, some tied to locations, some more random and some able to be triggered depending on players locations and actions. The event clocks and hexkeys serves as the main methods to trigger encounters. Hexkeys will generally contain a location with an associated encounter allowing that location to adopt new encounters over time. The event clocks should generate their events more unpredictably and with lower preperations required by using encounter tables for [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Exploration Encounter Table\|Exploration Encounter Table]] and delving into [[Hexcrawl Rules/Encounter Tables/Dungeon Encounter Table\|Locations]].
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Rules & Glossary/Hexcrawl Resting Rules\|Hexcrawl Resting Rules]]
Alternate resting rules can be used for a hexcrawl if the goal is to challenge the players in combat without having to use multiple encounters per day to drain resources or run high deadliness in each encounter. This reinforces the value of resource management in simpler encounters and tactics to preserve the health of characters. For the hexcrawl this means that what and how you chose to explore comes at a greater opportunity cost and becomes more meaningful.
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Sanctuary/Sanctuary Rules\|Sanctuary Rules]] 
The sanctuary or settlement that the players use as a base of operations, usually a town or village. This serves as a place to effectively recover health and resources without risk and store your earnings. The sanctuary can also be a place to invest your earnings to open new shops, improve the quality and quantity of offered supplies and items as well as provide hirelings. Can be combined with Bastions.
#### [[Hexcrawl Rules/Sanctuary/Hirelings/Hireling Rules\|Hireling Rules]]
Hirelings can be used as support during the players adventures or as a means of allowing expansion and defense of allied territory to be done using gold and loot instead of player time.
